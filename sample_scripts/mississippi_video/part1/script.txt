BEATS (VO exactly):
1. Paris, 1715. France is bankrupt from war, and a smooth-talking Scottish gambler arrives with a dangerous idea, paper money.
2. John Law is a math genius and a card shark, wanted for killing a man in a duel. The Regent of France lets him open a bank anyway.
3. His trick, the bank prints notes backed by confidence instead of gold. France loves it, and the economy roars back to life.
4. Then Law merges his bank with the Mississippi Company, owner of all trade with Louisiana, and whispers spread, there is gold everywhere.
5. In 1719, the shares go from five hundred livres to eighteen thousand. Paris invents a brand new word, the millionaire.
6. The street outside his office becomes a human casino. A hunchback famously rents out his own back as a desk for signing share papers.
7. Duchesses fight for shares, servants turn into lords, and Law becomes richer than the king himself. On paper, anyway.
8. Then one morning, the Prince de Conti arrives with three wagons, demanding real gold for his paper notes. And everyone starts wondering.
